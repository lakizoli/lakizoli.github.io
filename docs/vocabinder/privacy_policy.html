Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

📋 1. Overview

VocaBinder (the "App") is designed with your privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

💾 2. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your dictionary entries, language pairs, notes, and learning statistics are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

🔑 3. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use its features.

🤖 4. AI Assistant

The App includes an AI Assistant that can suggest vocabulary corrections and translations, analyze your learning statistics, and chat with you for practice. You choose which of three backends powers the Assistant from a picker in the Assistant panel.

On-Device (the default for every user) runs entirely on your device using Apple's Foundation Models and Translation frameworks; nothing is sent to Apple's servers or any third party. Switching to either of the other two backends always requires you to select it explicitly — the App never switches backends on its own.

Apple Private Cloud Compute (iOS 27 or later) sends your chat messages and the specific vocabulary-table context needed to answer them — such as individual lookups, missing-translation lists, duplicate pairs, or statistics summaries, never your full vocabulary table — to Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers for processing, under Apple's own published privacy architecture, which Apple states is independently verifiable and does not retain your data after your request is served.

Gemini (iOS 27 or later) sends the same kind of chat messages and vocabulary-table context to Google's Gemini API, reached via Google's Firebase platform, for processing. This integration is currently offered by Google in Preview status, so its availability or behavior could change without notice. Google's own terms for this API distinguish free and paid usage tiers with different data-retention and data-use policies; please review Google's current Gemini API terms if you want the specifics, since the App does not control them.

Before the App uses either Private Cloud Compute or Gemini for the first time, it shows a one-time notice naming Apple or Google and explaining that your data will leave your device, which you must accept before that backend takes effect. Regardless of which backend is active, the Assistant never edits your vocabulary data automatically — it only proposes changes, which you must confirm before anything is applied — and chat conversations are not saved; they are discarded as soon as you close the Assistant panel. An app-side content safety filter screens Assistant input and output across all three backends; Apple's additional on-device safety guardrails apply only to the on-device backend.

🌐 5. Third-Party Services

When you use the translation features, the App loads web pages from third-party services such as Google Translate and online dictionaries directly in an embedded browser. These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies. The App itself does not send your data to these services programmatically — it only opens their web pages with your search terms.

💳 6. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional tip purchases through Apple's StoreKit framework. All transactions are handled by Apple. The developer does not have access to your payment information or Apple ID.

📈 7. Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use any analytics frameworks, crash reporting tools, or advertising trackers. No usage data is collected or shared with third parties.

🗄️ 8. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the App, all associated data will be permanently removed. The App supports standard iOS backup mechanisms through iCloud or local backups.

🛡️ 9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is safe for users of all ages.

🔄 10. Changes to This Policy

The developer reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected in the App with an updated revision date.

✉️ 11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store listing.
