Privacy: Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application ("the App").

📋 Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personally identifiable information.

💳 In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Payment processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy and terms of service.

📢 Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may use device identifiers to serve relevant ads. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.

🏆 Game Data and Leaderboards

Game progress and scores are stored locally on your device. The App utilizes Apple's native GameKit and Game Center services to provide global leaderboards. When you participate, your gameplay scores and Game Center username are processed and stored entirely by Apple. We do not collect, store, or transmit this data to any of our own servers or third parties. Your use of Game Center is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

🛡️ Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.

✉️ Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store support link.
