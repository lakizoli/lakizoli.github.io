⚡ How It Works

Blocks are dropping from the top. The grid is packed with numbers. Your mission? Find the exact spot on the board where the covered cells sum up to the target number.

⭐ The Star

Panicking? Use the star hint to instantly reveal where you can find appropriate sums on the field.

💣 The Bomb

Blow up cells upon touch for 3 seconds to clear out problematic numbers and reset your layout.

🧽 The Sponge

Swipe the field for 3 seconds to wipe out messy areas and clean the slate.
